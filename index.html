About the Author

Kenneth H. Otto

I am a musician, writer, and follower of Christ based in Alberta, Canada. For years I explored the intersection of science, spirituality, and Scripture — searching for a framework that made sense of manifestation, faith, and purpose without the hype of prosperity gospel or the emptiness of pure self-help.

I found my answer in sympathetic resonance. Not a new-age concept — a physical law. One that points, quietly and powerfully, back to the Creator who spoke the universe into existence.

My first book, The Law of Resonant Alignment, is the result of that journey. I write for believers and seekers alike who are tired of formulas and ready for something grounded in truth.
