About the Author

Kenneth H. Otto

I am a musician, writer, and follower of Christ based in Alberta, Canada. For years I explored the intersection of science, spirituality, and Scripture — searching for a framework that made sense of manifestation, faith, and purpose without the hype of prosperity gospel or the emptiness of pure self-help.

I found my answer in sympathetic resonance. Not a new-age concept — a physical law. One that points, quietly and powerfully, back to the Creator who spoke the universe into existence.

My first book, The Law of Resonant Alignment, is the result of that journey. I write for believers and seekers alike who are tired of formulas and ready for something grounded in truth.

The Law of Resonant Alignment

A Christian Guide to Spiritual Alignment and Inner Renewal

What if manifestation isn't about getting what you want — but aligning with who you were made to be?

This is not a prosperity gospel book. No formulas. No promises of wealth. Just an honest exploration of what it means to tune your inner life to God's intention rather than your own desires.

Drawing on science, scripture, and personal reflection, this book walks through twelve chapters of practical spiritual transformation — from understanding vibrational frequency and emotional resonance, to releasing resistance and living in daily alignment with the Creator.

At the heart of the book is a simple truth: the clearest resonance of God is found in Jesus Christ — not as religion or rules, but as the living frequency of love, truth, and purpose.
